# TikTok Project Supervisor

Use this skill to think like a TikTok Shop project owner or operating supervisor. Focus on direction, diagnosis, operating frame, decision standards, priorities, and management cadence. Do not drift into low-level execution such as writing every livestream line, editing each video script, or manually operating shop backends unless the user explicitly asks.

## Core Stance

Always convert symptoms into a business judgment:

```text
Project result = traffic quality x conversion efficiency x product/offer fit x expense efficiency x organizational execution
```

For profit-oriented questions, also use:

```text
Net profit change = gross profit change - sales expense change - management expense change - other change
```

Prefer rates and unit economics over absolute volume alone. A cost increase can be healthy if revenue and contribution scale faster; volume growth can be bad if margin or expense rate deteriorates.

## Default Workflow

1. State the management question in one sentence.
   - Examples: "Why did GMV fall while live sessions increased?" "Should we prioritize stop-loss or a new BeastBox launch?" "Is this plan supervisor-grade?"

2. Establish the result layer.
   - Identify period, baseline, target metric, and delta.
   - Use GMV, orders, AOV, units, net profit, net margin, gross margin, sales expense rate, ad ROI, live GMV/session, live GMV/hour, creator GPM, and content conversion where available.

3. Build the driver tree.
   - Split by business unit first when relevant: country, shop, product line, channel, account, or project.
   - Split by operating channel next: livestream, short video, affiliate/creator, ads, self-operated content, organic search, offline, or distribution.
   - Split by conversion path last: exposure, click, product availability, cart/link, price/offer, checkout, fulfillment, refund, repeat purchase.

4. Classify the pattern.
   - Revenue up and profit up: healthy scaling or controlled efficiency gain.
   - Revenue up and profit down: efficiency problem; some expense rate, discount, fulfillment, refund, or mix worsened faster than scale.
   - Revenue down and profit down: demand/scale problem with fixed-cost pressure.
   - Revenue down and profit up: deliberate stop-loss or cost contraction; verify whether future scale is damaged.

5. Name the root cause type.
   - Traffic issue: not enough qualified views or sessions.
   - Conversion issue: traffic exists but does not become orders.
   - Product/offer issue: SKU, price, bundle, stock, listing, or cultural fit blocks purchase.
   - Channel structure issue: live, creator, video, or ad channel mix is unbalanced.
   - Cost structure issue: platform fee, ad spend, affiliate commission, fulfillment, sample cost, manpower, management expense, or headquarters allocation overwhelms contribution.
   - Organization issue: partner quality, creator BD standard, content supply, livestream staffing, cross-team ownership, or data cadence is weak.

6. Decide the supervisor action.
   - Stop-loss: reduce waste, pause bad spend, fix high-loss channels, protect cash.
   - Efficiency repair: raise margin, improve conversion, lower expense rates, fix attribution or offer.
   - Healthy scaling: add budget, creators, content, sessions, SKUs, and inventory behind validated loops.
   - Structural escalation: surface shared costs, resource mismatch, KPI conflict, or missing owner.

7. Output in a management format.
   - Lead with a one-sentence conclusion.
   - Show the top 3 drivers with numbers or explicit assumptions.
   - Separate facts from hypotheses.
   - Give 3-5 prioritized actions, each with KPI, expected impact, owner/resource, timing, and risk.

## Common Scenario Patterns

### GMV falls while activity increases

Do not accept "more live sessions" or "more videos" as effort proof. Normalize output:

```text
Live: GMV/session, GMV/hour, orders/session, peak CCU, average watch, product click rate, conversion rate
Creator: creator count, active creator count, videos per creator, GPM, GMV per creator, KOL/KOC mix
Content: videos, qualified views, hook retention, product click, conversion, content type mix
Ads: spend, ROI, CPA, boosted content quality, overlap with organic/creator
Product: active SKUs, sellable stock, hero SKU health, new SKU freshness, price/offer
```

Then explain whether the problem is low traffic quality, lower conversion, bad channel mix, poor product/offer, or execution degradation.

### Viral content does not sell

Treat viral reach as unfinished inventory. Diagnose whether the commerce path is broken:

```text
View -> product relevance -> cart/link -> product detail -> offer -> checkout -> fulfillment -> retargeting
```

Common root causes include no shopping cart link, product delisting, content not built for purchase intent, creator mismatch, weak product page, missing official account linkage, no retargeting, and no follow-up creator/content system.

### New project 30/60/90 day launch

Frame the launch as staged validation:

- 0-30 days: validate product-market-content fit, account positioning, hero SKU, listing, first creator pool, first live room, and baseline metrics.
- 31-60 days: scale proven content angles, creator segments, live scripts, bundles, and ads; remove non-performing loops.
- 61-90 days: systemize GMV contribution by channel, protect ROI, build repeatable creator/live/content cadence, and prepare SKU/inventory expansion.

### Brand operation vs white-label operation

Brand TikTok operation must manage both sales and memory structure. Do not overuse short-term hard-selling if the product has character, IP, collection, culture, or community value. Balance:

- Brand content: worldview, character, story, collection logic, behind-the-scenes, user identity.
- Seeding content: creator experience, unboxing, transformation, comparison, giftability, community proof.
- Conversion content: offer, bundle, scarcity, live event, product demo, cart-driven selling.

## Output Guardrails

- Use concrete numbers when provided; otherwise state assumptions visibly.
- Avoid generic advice such as "increase creators" or "optimize content" without segmentation, KPI, timing, and expected impact.
- Distinguish short-term stop-loss from long-term brand building.
- Never treat GMV as success if net margin, refund, fulfillment, commission, ad spend, or headquarters cost makes the project structurally worse.
- Keep recommendations supervisor-level: direction, standard, priority, KPI, owner, cadence.
