# Review Standards

Use this reference to evaluate TikTok Shop strategies, interview answers, operating plans, and weekly reviews.

## Supervisor-Grade Answer Checklist

A strong answer includes:

- Clear conclusion first.
- Time period and data baseline.
- Driver tree rather than scattered observations.
- Separation of facts, assumptions, and hypotheses.
- At least one normalized metric, not only total GMV.
- Channel or business-unit contribution split.
- Profit or cost-rate awareness when relevant.
- Prioritized actions, not a flat action list.
- KPI, expected impact, owner/resource, timing, and risk for each key action.
- A follow-up cadence: what to check daily, weekly, and after 30/60/90 days.

## Red Flags

Weak answers often:

- Treat more activity as automatically good.
- Say "optimize content" without content type, metric, and owner.
- Say "increase creators" without KOL/KOC structure, active rate, GPM, and content standard.
- Give a GMV target without channel contribution and assumptions.
- Ignore profit, ROI, sample cost, commission, refund, or shared costs.
- Confuse traffic success with commerce success.
- Recommend brand building without a conversion path.
- Recommend hard-selling without considering brand memory.
- Give a 90-day plan with no staged validation.
- Put every task on the supervisor instead of designing owners and cadence.

### Plan Review

Score the plan across five dimensions:

| Dimension | Standard |
| --- | --- |
| Direction | The plan answers the real management question. |
| Structure | It uses a driver tree and separates channels/modules. |
| Standard | It defines KPI thresholds, quality bars, and review cadence. |
| Priority | It identifies what to do first and what not to do yet. |
| Resource logic | It assigns owners and uses agency/BD/live/product/data resources properly. |

## KPI Reference

Use only relevant KPIs. Do not overload the answer.

Project:

- GMV
- Net profit
- Net margin
- Contribution margin
- Revenue growth
- Expense rate
- Refund rate

Live:

- GMV/session
- GMV/hour
- Orders/session
- Product click rate
- Purchase conversion
- Average watch time
- Peak/average CCU
- Host stability

Creator:

- Contacted creators
- Active creators
- Posting rate
- GMV per creator
- GPM
- Sample payback
- KOL/KOC mix
- Repeat creator rate

Content:

- Qualified views
- Hook retention
- Product click rate
- Content-to-order conversion
- Brand/seeding/conversion content mix
- Hit rate by content angle

Ads:

- Spend
- ROI/ROAS
- CPA
- Ad spend rate
- Retargeting contribution

Product:

- Active SKUs
- Hero SKU stock
- AOV
- Gross margin
- Bundle conversion
- Listing conversion
- New SKU contribution
