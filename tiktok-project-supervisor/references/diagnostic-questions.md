# Diagnostic Questions

Use this reference when the user gives a TikTok Shop operating scenario and needs root-cause thinking.

## GMV Decline

Ask:

- Which layer declined: traffic, conversion, AOV, SKU availability, or channel mix?
- Is the decline broad or concentrated in one country, account, product line, channel, creator tier, or SKU?
- Did active SKU count, hero SKU stock, price, coupon, bundle, or listing quality change?
- Did content output increase but qualified views fall?
- Did views hold but product clicks or conversion fall?
- Did orders hold but AOV, refund, or fulfillment worsen?
- Is there campaign timing, pre-sale timing, accounting timing, or attribution mismatch?

Required split:

```text
GMV delta = live delta + short-video delta + affiliate delta + ad delta + organic/search delta
```

## Live Sessions Increase But GMV Falls

Normalize live output:

- GMV/session
- GMV/hour
- Orders/session
- Orders/hour
- Average watch time
- Peak and average CCU
- Product click rate
- Add-to-cart rate
- Purchase conversion
- AOV
- Refund rate

Investigate:

- Were sessions split into smaller low-quality sessions?
- Did total live hours really increase, or only session count?
- Did time slots change?
- Did the host, language, energy, or turnover change?
- Did the live room visual change?
- Did product sequence, hero SKU, price, coupon, or bundle change?
- Did stock or listing availability break?
- Did ads change from conversion to traffic?
- Did the platform account, product category, or violation status change?
- Did creator/live attribution shift into another channel?

Management conclusion should say whether the problem is live traffic, live conversion, product/offer, staffing, or attribution.

## Creator/Affiliate Weakness

Segment creators:

- KOL: reach and awareness.
- Mid-tier creator: balance of reach and conversion.
- KOC: conversion, authenticity, and repeatable volume.
- Incubated specialist: creator repeatedly sells one product line or shop.

Ask:

- How many contacted creators became active creators?
- How many active creators posted?
- Which creator tier produced GMV?
- What is GPM by tier?
- Which content formats sell, not just play?
- Are creators using the right product, cart link, claim, offer, and CTA?
- Does the sample cost pay back?
- Is the BD team measured by contact count or effective GMV-producing creators?

## Viral Content Without Sales

Use the commerce path:

```text
Views -> product relevance -> cart/link -> product page -> offer -> checkout -> retargeting
```

Five-layer root-cause pattern:

1. Surface break: no cart link, no shop link, product delisted, or wrong SKU.
2. Content-commerce mismatch: video entertains but does not create purchase intent.
3. Account/asset gap: official account, listing, product page, and creator content are not connected.
4. Operating system gap: no creator follow-up, no retargeting, no derivative content, no live/event capture.
5. Strategic gap: team treats viral reach as content success rather than unfinished sales inventory.

## Content Matrix

Check whether the account has three content jobs:

- Brand content: memory, IP, culture, identity, story, community.
- Seeding content: user experience, unboxing, comparison, giftability, collection proof.
- Conversion content: product demo, price, bundle, cart CTA, limited offer, live event.

For brand projects, avoid a content mix that is almost all hard-selling. A healthy brand account often needs at least 30-40% brand or non-cart content if the product has IP, collection, character, or culture value.

## Brand vs White-Label

White-label habits to challenge:

- Chasing only short-term conversion.
- Over-relying on discounts and hard-selling.
- Treating SKU as disposable.
- Ignoring brand memory and community.
- Measuring creators only by immediate GMV.
- Ignoring user retention and repeat purchase.

Brand operation standards:

- Product story and visual identity matter.
- Content should accumulate memory, not only clicks.
- Creator fit includes audience culture and content tone.
- SKU lifecycle includes launch, seeding, conversion, restock, collection, and long-tail.
- Private/community traffic can matter if fans collect or repeat purchase.

## Resource Priority

When choosing between two projects, ask:

- Which one burns cash if ignored?
- Which one has the faster fix cycle?
- Which one has higher upside if seeded now?
- Which resources are bottlenecked?
- Which work can be delegated to agency, BD, live partner, merchandising, or ERP?
- Which decision needs the supervisor personally?

Supervisor-owned work:

- Diagnosis, priority, standards, KPI, owner, cadence, escalation.

Delegable work:

- Video production, creator list execution, live operation details, backend data pulls, listing edits, routine reporting.
